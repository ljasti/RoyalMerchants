Structural concept, technical drawings (representation), visuals, branding, and manufacturing notes

Structural Concept

Exterior 3D View

A semi-arched German hanger roof with hot-galvanized steel trusses spans over expandable container modules. Roof purlins support waterproof panels, with wind bracing at end bays. The house modules dock under the hanger, allowing expansion and trailer compatibility.

Wind resistance Stable load paths Fast install

Interior Layout

Expandable modules create living spaces: bedroom, living/dining, kitchenette, and bathroom. Optional terrace or mezzanine sits under the hanger roof. Materials: 75mm EPS/rockwool sandwich panels; floor: 18mm MgO + 15mm laminate + PVC.

Human scale included for proportion.

Materials & Manufacturing Notes

Material Specifications

Structure: 2.5mm hot-galvanized steel frame; 4mm corner casts
Walls: 75mm EPS or rockwool sandwich panels; Fire rating A-grade
Floor: 18mm MgO + 15mm laminate + PVC protective layer
Roof: Waterproof hot-galvanized steel + anti-condensation layer; purlins at 1.2–1.5m spacing
Finish options: Bamboo fiber panels, UV decorative panels, terrace decking

Manufacturing & Installation

Welding: MIG with fillet welds (6–8mm) on truss nodes, continuous seam on frame members; galvanized post-weld coating
Steel thickness: Primary truss chords 3.0–4.0mm CFS/BTS; secondary members 2.0–2.5mm
Electrical + plumbing: pre-installed conduits, quick-connect manifolds; distribution board integrated
Installation time: 1–2 hours per module; hanger bolting on pre-set pedestals
Trailer-compatible: foldable modules with tie-down points and jack stands

Transport Volume (40HQ Container)

20ft expandable units (folded): 6–8 per 40HQ (typical vendor packing)
30ft expandable units (folded): 4–5 per 40HQ
40ft expandable units (folded): 3–4 per 40HQ
Hanger truss kits: nested arch segments and purlins shipped flat; bolted on site
