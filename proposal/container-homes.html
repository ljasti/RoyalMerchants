Materials & Manufacturing

Material Specifications

Frame: 2.5mm hot-galvanized steel; corner casts: 4mm
Walls: 75mm EPS or rockwool sandwich panels; Fire rating A-grade
Floor build-up: 18mm MgO + 15mm laminate + PVC protective layer
Roof: waterproof galvanized steel with anti-condensation layer
Finishes: bamboo fiber, UV decorative panels, optional terrace decking

Manufacturing & Deployment

Electrical & plumbing: pre-installed conduits; quick-connect manifolds; integrated distribution board
Installation: 1–2 hours per module; fold, lock, connect services
Trailer-ready: tie-down points, jack stands; safe transport configuration
Transport volume (40HQ): 20ft (6–8 units), 30ft (4–5), 40ft (3–4)
